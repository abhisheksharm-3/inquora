Every surface, one language

Substance left, apparatus right. The rule that made the chat screen work generalises: whatever the surface is about goes on the left, and everything that supports, explains or records it goes in the right column. Nine surfaces, one grammar.

It also settles the open question. The document viewer replaces the text column, not the apparatus. Click a specimen and the left side becomes the page it came from, marked. The apparatus never moves, so the layout never becomes three columns.

Shown in light except the landing page, which commits to dark because the brand register is allowed a statement the product register is not. Both themes ship for every surface.

07 Resolves the open question

Following a citation

revenue-review-q3.pdf · page 4 of 14Back to the answer

Pipeline coverage is reported weekly and reviewed against the 3.0x standard set in the annual plan. Throughout Q3 the reported figure did not fall below 3.2x in any week, and peaked at 3.9x in the second week of August.

Coverage remained above the 3.0x target throughout the quarter. Coverage is calculated on unweighted opportunity value, which does not adjust for the observed decline in average deal size.

The finance team has raised this definition previously. A weighted coverage measure was proposed in the Q1 review and deferred pending a change to the reporting pipeline, which remains outstanding.

Opportunity count grew 18% quarter on quarter, which explains the stability of the ratio in a quarter where closed value fell materially short of plan.

Specimens become numbered footnotes below the answer, still tappable from the mark in the text. Operations collapse to one line of record at the top of the turn, because on a phone nobody reads four timings. The document viewer takes the whole screen and one tap returns.

What this settles, and what it does not

Settled: the grammar. Substance left, apparatus right, on every surface. Newsreader carries anything read, IBM Plex Mono carries anything recorded. One oxide mark, adjusted for lightness per theme. Citations reachable in one action, and returnable in one more.

  1. The tablet range, roughly 700 to 1150px, is still unanswered. Too narrow for two columns, too wide for footnotes to feel right. The likely answer is footnotes with a wider measure, but it needs building before anyone believes it.
  2. Operations accumulate forever. Twenty turns in, the apparatus is mostly timings. Proposal: operations fade to a single summary line once a turn completes, specimens persist.
  3. The second register is still typographic only. Serif against mono is a real collision, and it is quiet. The empty state is where something drawn could earn its place, and I have deliberately not added it yet rather than decorate.
  4. The landing page has one screen. What follows the fold is unwritten. The apparatus device could run the length of the page, which would be distinctive, or become exhausting.
